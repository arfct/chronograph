Privacy Policy

Chronograph does not collect your data.

Chronograph reads your calendar, reminders and chosen time zones on your device in order to draw them in a widget. That information stays on the device. We do not collect it, store it on servers, share it, sell it, or use it for advertising. There are no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

What the app accesses, and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Calendar events To render your upcoming events as text in the widget and in-app preview. Stays on device. Read when the widget refreshes; never transmitted.
Reminders (Apple platforms) To render incomplete reminders in the widget. Stays on device; never transmitted.
Chosen time zones Your widget configuration — which cities to show. Stored on device in app settings.
Approximate location (Apple platforms, optional) Only if you enable the weather feature, to fetch a local forecast. Sent to Apple's WeatherKit to return a forecast. Not stored by us, not linked to an identity, and never sent anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR on Android, Calendars on Apple platforms) — read-only. Chronograph never creates, edits or deletes events.
  • Reminders (Apple platforms) — read-only.
  • Location (Apple platforms, optional) — only requested if you turn on weather. Declining leaves every other feature working.

The Android app requests no network permission at all, so it is incapable of sending anything off the device.

No accounts, no third parties

Chronograph has no sign-in, no user accounts and no server of its own. It contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs. The only network request the app can ever make is the optional weather lookup described above, which goes to Apple.

Children

Chronograph is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your widget settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Calendar and reminder data belongs to your operating system's calendar and reminders apps — Chronograph only reads it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what the app accesses, the effective date below will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Chronograph? Use the developer contact details on the app's App Store or Google Play listing, which are kept current there.
