Chronograph is a set of widgets for the home screen and lock screen. They show calendar events, reminders and the time in other cities as plain text, set in the system fonts and tinted with the device's accent color. There are no dials, gauges or illustrations.

Two obfuscation modes, Braille and Enigma, make the agenda hard to read for anyone looking over the owner's shoulder. The Android version arrived in 2026; reminders are available only on the Apple platforms. On iOS it is in TestFlight and has not shipped on the App Store.

Swift for iOS, macOS and watchOS; Kotlin and Glance for Android. 2024.
